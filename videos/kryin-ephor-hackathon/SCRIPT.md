Kryin Ephor is the operating system for modern schools.

Schools should not have to stitch together disconnected tools for people,
academics, attendance, finance, and learning. Kryin Ephor brings those workflows
into one connected canvas.

At the platform level, Super Admins can oversee schools, manage tenants, and keep
an eye on system health from one dashboard. At the school level, administrators
manage users, roles, classes, and daily operations without losing context.

Attendance becomes a quick, visible daily loop. Finance brings fee plans,
invoices, receipts, and salary records together in the same system.

For students, the experience stays personal: a clear dashboard, upcoming tests,
progress tracking, and a distraction-free Focus Mode for one task at a time.

Under the hood, the project is designed as a multi-tenant SaaS platform, with 54
tables, 211 row-level security policies, and seven role-specific experiences.

Codex and GPT-5.6 were used as development collaborators: turning product
requirements into interface work, helping structure role-based flows, and speeding
up the iteration from idea to working experience.

Next, Kryin Ephor will expand with the parent portal, mobile-first workflows,
advanced analytics, exports, and richer school communication.

One intelligent system, built for every school role.
