## Video direction

Premium education-technology film: warm ivory interface cards sit on a near-black
grid, with emerald as the operational signal. Every supplied product screen is
treated as evidence, not decoration. Text is large enough for YouTube playback;
captions are concise scene headers rather than a dense transcript. Camera movement
uses deterministic zoom and pan over the supplied screenshots, with clean cuts and
brief editorial title cards.
