## Video direction

Premium education-technology launch film. Warm ivory and dark stone create trust; emerald is the
operational signal for active workflows, status, and AI. Real product screenshots are treated as
evidence. Motion uses smooth GSAP-style camera moves, UI zoom-ins, masked screenshot pans, kinetic
typography, deterministic data count-ups, and glassy dark panels. Reveals are paced to the narration;
no front-loaded slideshow, no copyrighted music, no third-party logo wall.
